Our story

We’re building a more generous way to learn.

SkillSwap was created for students who want growth without barriers, where knowledge becomes a shared resource instead of a paid service.

Our story

We noticed that many students had valuable skills but little access to affordable learning. SkillSwap turns that gap into an opportunity by connecting people who want to teach with people who want to learn.

Mission

Make peer-to-peer learning accessible, practical, and empowering for every student.

Vision

Create a campus culture where sharing knowledge feels natural, rewarding, and community-driven.

Why choose SkillSwap

Built for students, designed for momentum.

Free to join

Flexible exchange

Supportive community
